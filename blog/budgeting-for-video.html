How to Budget for Wedding Videography in DFW

By Eli Shepherd | July 3, 2025

In the U.S., the average couple spends about $1,799 on wedding videography, with most budgets ranging between $1,000 and $2,500 ([weddingwire.com](https://www.weddingwire.com/cost/wedding-videographer?utm_source=chatgpt.com)). In the Dallas–Fort Worth area, you can expect prices to run slightly higher, often averaging around $2,300 for a full highlight package ([theknot.com](https://www.theknot.com/content/average-cost-wedding-videographer?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).

1. Determine Your Total Wedding Budget

Decide on your overall wedding budget first. A common rule of thumb is to allocate 10–15% of that total for photography and videography combined ([xpressionvideo.com](https://www.xpressionvideo.com/wd/blog/lights-camera-wedding-the-ultimate-budgeting-guide-for-videography-services?utm_source=chatgpt.com)). For example, on a $30,000 wedding, reserving $3,000–$4,500 for video ensures quality coverage without sacrificing other essentials.

2. Understand Package Components

Most videography packages include hours of coverage, number of cameras, drone footage, and editing. Extra services—like raw footage delivery or same-day edits—often incur additional fees. List your “must-haves” vs. “nice-to-haves” to prioritize spending.

3. Ask for Custom Quotes

Instead of one-size-fits-all pricing, request a custom proposal that breaks down each service’s cost. Transparent quotes help you negotiate and eliminate hidden fees.
