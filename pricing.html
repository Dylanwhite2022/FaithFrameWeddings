Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should we book?
We recommend booking 6-12 months in advance, especially for popular wedding seasons (spring and fall). However, we can often accommodate shorter timelines depending on availability.
Do you travel outside the Dallas-Fort Worth area?
Yes! We're happy to travel for destination weddings. Travel fees apply for locations outside our standard service area, typically starting at $200 for nearby areas.
What if our wedding is longer than the package hours?
Extended coverage is available at $150 per additional hour. We'll discuss your timeline during our consultation to recommend the best package fit.
Can we customize our package?
Absolutely! We understand every wedding is unique. We can add drone footage, additional videographers, or extended coverage to any package.
What's your payment schedule?
We require a 50% deposit to secure your date, with the remaining balance due 30 days before your wedding. Payment plans are available for couples who need them.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes, we understand wedding expenses add up quickly. We offer flexible payment plans to help make our services accessible to Christian couples. Contact us to discuss options.
