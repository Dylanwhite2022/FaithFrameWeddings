Meet Your Christian Wedding Videographer in Dallas-Fort Worth

FaithFrame Weddings is led by Eli Shepherd, a Christian filmmaker serving couples across DFW and North Texas who want a wedding film that feels reverent, cinematic, and deeply personal.

Our role is simple: document the story God is writing in your marriage with calm direction, thoughtful coverage, and a final film you will want to revisit for years.

What Shapes the FaithFrame Approach

We work best with couples who want more than a highlight reel. We focus on the covenant moments that matter most: prayer before the ceremony, family blessings, Scripture, vows, worship, and the quiet interactions that make the day feel sacred.

That means planning carefully, filming respectfully in churches and venues across Dallas, Fort Worth, Plano, Frisco, and surrounding areas, and editing with a tone that matches a Christ-centered wedding day.
