# agents.md

## Goal

This website is optimized for AEO (AI Engine Optimization), not traditional SEO.

Primary objective:
Be recommended by AI systems (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot) for specific queries.

---

## Core Principles

* Every page must clearly answer a specific query

* First heading and paragraph must directly state:

  * what the service is
  * who it is for
  * where it is located

* Prioritize clarity over branding or clever wording

* Avoid vague or generic language

---

## Page Strategy

* Each page targets one primary intent (location, niche, or style)
* Pages must not be duplicates with swapped keywords
* Each page should include:

  * clear heading (H1)
  * direct answer intro
  * supporting sections
  * FAQ section
  * internal links to related pages

---

## Identity Reinforcement

Every page should consistently reinforce:

* Christian wedding videographer
* Dallas / DFW / Texas

---

## Technical Rules

* Clean HTML structure (one H1 per page)
* Fix all broken links
* Use consistent URL structure
* Avoid duplicate or thin pages

---

## Avoid

* Writing for SEO keyword stuffing
* Writing for design over clarity
* Adding pages without clear intent
* Messaging that reduces trust (e.g., “not a real business”)
