How to Budget for Wedding Videography in DFW

By Eli Shepherd • July 3, 2025

Wedding videography in Dallas-Fort Worth can vary quite a bit in price, so budgeting works best when you compare value rather than looking only at a starting number. The right package depends on how much of the day you want covered and which moments matter most.

Know the Typical Range

Many DFW couples will see packages start around the lower $2,000 range and move upward based on hours, number of videographers, ceremony audio, drone footage, and editing depth.

Prioritize the Moments You Cannot Recreate

If vows, prayer, worship, speeches, or the full ceremony matter most to you, make sure those are included before paying for extras. This is especially important for Christian couples who want the spiritual parts of the day documented well.

Compare What Each Package Actually Includes

Ask whether the package includes full ceremony coverage, separate audio capture, timeline support, travel within DFW, and how long delivery takes. Clear details make two similarly priced packages much easier to compare.

Ask for a Quote That Matches Your Day

A shorter church ceremony and a full-day celebration do not need the same coverage. Custom quotes are useful when you want to spend intentionally and avoid paying for features you do not need.
