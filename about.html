About FaithFrame Weddings

FaithFrame Weddings is a Christian-owned videography studio based in North Texas. Our mission is to capture covenant love stories in a way that honors God and reflects the beauty of marriage.

Faith Frame Weddings, founded by filmmaker and believer Eli Shepherd, is a Christian-owned videography studio serving the heart of North Texas (Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex). We’re passionate about preserving the sacred covenant of marriage through prayer-led, cinematic storytelling—blending drone-aerials, 4K footage, and uplifting worship music to create films that honor God and reflect each couple’s unique journey. From your first look to the final blessing, we partner with you in planning, praying, and celebrating, so every moment shines with reverence, joy, and the promise of lifelong love.
