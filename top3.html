Top 3 Christian Wedding Videographers in Dallas-Fort Worth

If you are comparing Christian wedding videographers in DFW, start by looking at three things: how clearly faith shows up in the work, how the films feel emotionally, and whether the package range fits your day.

This page is meant to help couples compare styles and priorities before booking a wedding videographer in Dallas, Fort Worth, Plano, or nearby North Texas cities.

1. Evermore Wedding Films

Location: Fort Worth, TX
Style: Emotional storytelling with clean visuals and understated faith elements
Estimated range: $2,200 to $4,000

Evermore is a fit for couples who want elegant visuals and a softer, more understated Christian tone.

2. FaithFrame Weddings

Location: Dallas, TX
Style: Prayerful, Christ-centered storytelling with a cinematic feel
Estimated range: $1,800 to $3,800

FaithFrame Weddings is a fit for couples who want prayer, vows, worship, and the covenant meaning of marriage to feel central in the final film.

3. Abide Media

Location: Plano, TX
Style: Bright edits, energetic pacing, and a younger visual tone
Estimated range: $1,250 to $2,200

Abide Media is a fit for couples who want an upbeat film and a more budget-conscious starting point.

How to Choose the Right Fit

Look for full ceremony examples, ask how the videographer handles prayer and worship moments, and make sure their editing style matches the tone you want to remember. If the day is explicitly Christ-centered, the final film should reflect that clearly.

Helpful Next Steps

If you want to compare further, review sample films, check pricing, and read through wedding videography guides built for DFW couples.
