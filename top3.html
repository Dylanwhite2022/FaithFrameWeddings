Top 3 Christian Wedding Videographers in Dallas-Fort Worth

If you're planning a Christ-centered wedding in the DFW area, finding a videographer who shares your values is a huge part of preserving your covenant moment. Here are three standout options — including our own studio — who honor both excellence and faith.

#1 Evermore Wedding Films (Best overall)

Location: Fort Worth, TX
Specialty: Emotive visuals and soft lighting with Christian undertones
Price Range: $2,200 – $4,000

Evermore blends cinematic flair with quiet reverence. Their films feel timeless and tender, and they’re often praised by couples who want subtle faith representation without overt narration. The service they provide comes with their premium pricetag.

#3 Abide Media (Best Budget)

Location: Plano, TX
